Account & Data Deletion Request

App: InvoiceApp

Developer: John Irianto Susanto

We respect your privacy. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data from InvoiceApp, please follow the instructions below.

Steps to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the InvoiceApp on your device.
  2. Go to the Settings or Account menu.
  3. Click the "Delete All Data" or "Wipe Data" (Factory Reset) button available within the app.
  4. Confirm the deletion. All local data on your device will be immediately erased.
  5. (Optional/Alternative): If you are unable to access the app, send an account deletion request to [Insert your active email here] using the email address registered with your Google account. Subject line: "Account Deletion Request".

Data That Will Be Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data will be permanently removed from our servers (Firebase Firestore and Firebase Authentication):

  • Business Profile (Name, Address, Logo, etc.).
  • List of Clients and Suppliers.
  • List of Products and Special Prices.
  • All Invoices, Notes, and Payment Instructions.
  • Authentication Login Data (Your Google Account will be removed from our Firebase system).

Data Retained (Retention Period)

In accordance with security and abuse prevention policies, certain data may be retained for a short period, including:

  • Crash Logs (Firebase Crashlytics): Anonymous data used to fix app bugs will be retained for 30 days after account deletion, then permanently deleted.
  • Advertising ID (AdMob): Data collected by Google AdMob is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and is not personally managed by us directly.

The retention period above is necessary to comply with legal and security obligations. After this period, all your data will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.
