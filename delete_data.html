Data Deletion Request

App: InvoiceApp

Developer: John Irianto Susanto

We give you full control over your data. You can request the deletion of specific parts of your data directly within the app, or request bulk deletion of all business data without having to delete your account.

Steps to Delete Data

  1. Delete Individual Items (In-App): Open InvoiceApp, go to the relevant list (Invoices, Clients, or Products), tap on the item, and select the "Delete" option. This will permanently remove that specific item from your local device and our cloud servers immediately.
  2. Delete All Business Data (In-App): Go to Settings and tap "Wipe Data" (or "Delete All Data"). This will remove all your invoices, clients, products, and payment instructions, but will keep your account logged in.
  3. Request Data Deletion via Email: If you cannot access the app, send an email to [Insert your active email here] with the subject "Data Deletion Request". Please include your registered Google email address and specify exactly which data you want removed (e.g., "Delete all invoices from 2025", or "Delete a specific client named X").

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Specific Invoices (if requested individually).
  • Specific Clients/Suppliers and their associated prices.
  • Specific Products and their stock information.
  • Business Profile (Name, Address, Logo, etc.) - *Only if requested via email for full data wipe.*

Data That Will Be Kept

  • Your Account Login details (Email/Google Account).
  • App Settings and Preferences.

Retention Period

Data deleted using the in-app buttons is removed immediately and permanently. For requests via email, data will be processed and permanently deleted within 30 days of receiving your request. Crash logs (Firebase Crashlytics) are automatically deleted after 30 days.
